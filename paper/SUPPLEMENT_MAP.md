# Supplement map: former appendix names to the merged supplement

`paper/supplementary.md` merges `supplementary_appendices.md`, `A2_diagnostics.md`, `A3_protocol.md` and
`S1_few_shot_recovery.md` (2026-09-23). Sections are numbered S1, S1.1, S1.1.1; tables are numbered
Table S1, S2 ... in order of appearance, independently of the sections. `paper/tex/verify_tex.mjs`
checks that every S-reference in the manuscript and the supplement resolves, and that no former
name remains. Parenthesised roman numerals, as in C.5(ix), carry over: S3.5(ix).
