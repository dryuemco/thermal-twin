2.1.3 Selection Rationale for Each AOI 
Manavgat 2021 : Firstly, AOI didn’t choose as a symmetric buffer around Manavgat’s center. It was selected to cover a larger area of the Taurus forests and scrublands. East border was narrowed to exclude the greenhouse/citrus belt. North border was extended to Akseki/Gündoğmuş side natural vegetation. East/West borders cover neighboring forests while ensuring they didn’t extend too far into urban area of Antalya. [VERIFIED]
Bejis 2022 : Bejis/Castellon wildfire chosen to comparable Mediterrarean transfer wildfire situation. Bbox can not specified as exact fire perimeter, defined as regional study rectangle. Consistency was checked using the AOI preview and MCD64A1 burned-landcover gate. The code doesn’t give enough reason as much as Manavgat. [PARTIALLY VERIFIED – detailed geographic edge-selection rationale unresolved]
Muğla 2021: The AOI was delineated for the purpose of including the 2021 fire regions in and around Marmaris, Bodrum, Milas and Köyceğiz, along with adjacent forests and shrublands. The bbox is an example of a region of study rectangle and not the actual fire boundary or even all of Muğla province. Geographic reasoning is established, but the history of when the geometry was set before looking at gates or performance results cannot be verified. STATUS: PARTIALLY VERIFIED – geographic reasoning verified; history of freeze not verified.
North Evia Extended 2021 : Extended AOI include old Evia AOI geometrically and extended to include the unburned natural vegetation around it and The Limni-Rovies-Agia Anna-Pefki corridor in northern Evia. The metadata specifically notes that the geometry was selected based solely on geographic coverage; it was not adjusted based on burned labels, prevalence, gate results, AUC/PR-AUC, or any other model metric. [VERIFIED]
Montiferru 2021 : Instead of choosing/drawing arbitrary wildfire-based bbox, ISTAT 2021 was derived from the combination of official municipal boundaries and rounded up. This approach is more defensible than the arbitrary bbox. [VERIFIED]

2.3.1 Analysis Unit and 510m Grid
The final modeling unit is not a 30-meter raster pixel.
Each modeling row is a single non-overlapping block reconstructed on the high-resolution reference raster to approximate nominal MCD64A1spatial scale.
Main reason is while exporting MCD64A1 BurnDate rasters on 30m resolution from Earth Engine same native MODIS information copies many 30m pixels.
If these 30m pixels used for independent observation same MCD64A1 cell may count many times. 
Step8A recreated MCD64A1 cell structure to solve this problem.
Status: VERIFIED
500m / 510m terminology:
The spatial resolution of the MCD64A1 product is referred to as ~500 m in the pipeline reports.
Reconstruction process within the pipeline was performed on a 30-meter reference raster.
Target native cell size 500m
By using 30m reference pixel: 
block_size = max(int(round(500.0 / 30.0)), 1) = 17
Therefore, the analysis cell in the application size is: 17x30 = 510m 
Status: COMPLETED
2.3.2 Native-grid reconstruction 
There is no separately stored, locally georeferenced native 500-meter MCD64A1 raster available.
Step6, exports MCD64A1 BurnDate 30m from Earth Engine.
This export repeats the native MODIS cell values on a 30 meter grid.
Step8A reconstructs nominal native grid by reducing 17x17 30m blocks to a single analysis cell.

30m sub-pixels are not independent model examples.

Exclude from eligible modeling population
Does not count as burned / unburned
It does not fall under the land-cover gate categories,
